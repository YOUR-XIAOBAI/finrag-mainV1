Helsinki Travel Guide

Top Attractions:
- Suomenlinna Sea Fortress (UNESCO site)
- Helsinki Cathedral (iconic white church)
- Market Square (Kauppatori) for local food
- Temppeliaukio Church (rock church)

Transportation:
- Excellent public transport (HSL system)
- Purchase day tickets for unlimited travel
- Ferries to Suomenlinna leave from Market Square

Local Tips:
- Try salmon soup at the market
- Visit during June for the Midnight Sun
- Free walking tours available daily

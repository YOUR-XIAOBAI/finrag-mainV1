Summer Travel (June-August)

Highlights:
- Midnight Sun (24h daylight in north)
- Midsummer celebrations (Juhannus)
- Music festivals (Flow, Ruisrock)
- Archipelago cruising

Activities:
- Hiking in national parks
- Berry picking (legal right under Everyman's Right)
- Lake swimming (thousands of lakes)
- Open-air museums

Packing Tips:
- Bring mosquito repellent
- Pack layers (nights can be cool)
- Sunglasses for midnight sun
